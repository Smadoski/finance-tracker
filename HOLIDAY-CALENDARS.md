# Working-day calendars in v2.7.0

Existing rules default to **Weekdays only**, preserving previous behaviour. Select a bank calendar when creating or editing a recurring rule. Exact-date rules ignore holidays.

**UK** means **England and Wales**, as labelled in the form; it does not include Scotland or Northern Ireland. The calendar computes standard annual bank holidays and substitute weekdays. It includes the exceptional dates for 2020, 2022 and 2023. The 2026 schedule was checked against [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays) on 9 September 2026.

**Cyprus** uses fixed bank holidays and Orthodox Easter offsets, including Clean Monday, Good Friday, Easter Monday and Tuesday, and Whit Monday. Saturdays and Sundays are non-working days. Reference: [Central Bank of Cyprus bank calendars](https://www.centralbank.cy/en/the-bank/working-hours-bank-holidays).

Calculations run offline. The arithmetic accepts 1900–2099, but this is not a complete historical holiday archive. Future exceptional closures require an application update; the app does not download calendar changes. Review unusual future dates against the official calendars.

For a salary nominally due Sunday 20 September 2026, Previous working day moves it to Friday 18 September. For UK Easter Monday 6 April 2026, it moves back across the weekend and Good Friday to Thursday 2 April.
