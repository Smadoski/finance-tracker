# Finance Tracker v2.6.0

## Whole-application responsive redesign

- New-item forms are collapsed until requested on Accounts, Transactions,
  Targets, Recurring Transactions and Valuations.
- Recurring rules, targets and pending entries use concise summaries with
  expandable management actions.
- Settings are grouped into focused expandable sections.
- Shared responsive cards, filter panels, status badges and management panels
  provide consistent desktop, iPad and iPhone behaviour.
- Dense tables convert to labelled mobile cards where appropriate, and the main
  navigation collapses into a touch-friendly menu at tablet/mobile widths.
- Quick Entry remains immediately available and focused on rapid capture.

## Compatibility

This is a user-interface release. It introduces no database migration and does
not change transaction signs, balances, transfers, recurrence calculations,
target calculations, reports, backups, Dropbox, authentication or user roles.
